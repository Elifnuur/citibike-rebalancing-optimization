# Citi Bike Rebalancing Optimization

## Scope

This project models station rebalancing as a minimum-cost network-flow problem using the 750-record benchmark in `data/sample_trips.csv`. The sample-derived station balance is arrivals minus departures; it is not a measurement of physical dock capacity or a complete operating-period demand forecast.

The verified run produced a full 817-station ledger: 364 supply, 331 demand, and 122 balanced stations. Coordinates were resolved for 814 stations; the 694-node optimization network contains 364 supply and 330 demand nodes. Stations `6644.07`, `8582.09`, and `4617.01` were unresolved and retained only in the ledger. The model transferred 463 bikes across 420 active pairs with a 255.32 bike-km objective. Weighted average distance was 0.551 km/bike; simple average 0.563 km/pair; median 0.446 km; minimum 0.060 km; maximum 2.596 km.

## Coordinate validation

Station IDs are handled as canonical strings. Coordinates are resolved from an optional authoritative station table when available. Otherwise, each station’s observations are evaluated against a 500-metre medoid neighbourhood. Only a dominant geographic cluster is averaged. If contradictory observations do not produce a dominant cluster, the station is retained in the balance ledger with `coordinate_status = unresolved` and is excluded from the distance matrix. This preserves flow accounting without inventing a location.

## Optimization model

For supply stations $i$ and demand stations $j$, $x_{ij}$ is the number of bikes transferred. The objective is

$$\min \sum_{i,j} c_{ij}x_{ij}$$

subject to supply and demand caps and a total flow equal to the smaller resolved supply or demand total. Integer flow follows from the total-unimodularity structure of the transportation formulation.

The cost $c_{ij}$ is Haversine distance in kilometres. It is a straight-line geographic distance, not an actual road distance, vehicle route distance, or travel time.

## Reproducibility

Run `julia --project=. -e "using Pkg; Pkg.instantiate()"`, then `julia --project=. src/rebalance_optimization.jl` and `julia --project=. test/runtests.jl`. The CSV outputs are generated by the model and must not be manually edited.

## Limitations

The benchmark is small and sample-derived. It does not model rack capacities, time windows, vehicle capacities, driver routes, road networks, or operational labor costs. A production study would add authoritative station metadata and a vehicle-routing layer.
